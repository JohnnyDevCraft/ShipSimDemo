SYSTEMS

Power reaches the systems in this order. When the reactor and battery cannot cover them all, the last ones get less: the cannon charges slower, the torpedo loads slower, the shield recharges slower, and the engine pushes less hard. The dampener needs all of its power or the engine stops.

Tap a key to change it, then press the new key. Escape cancels; Backspace leaves the command without a key. Keys are kept in this browser.

Space also fires the cannon, and + and − zoom, unless those keys are bound here. Joystick (first pass, standard mapping): stick turns and throttles; trigger or button 1 fires the cannon, button 2 launches a torpedo, button 3 (hold) is forward thrust, button 4 is all stop.
