# Tactical Officer Control

The tactical officer's console (2026-10-29), on the Helm design. It replaces the Angular weapons demo's TOC page (`demo-app` `weapons-demo`, route `/demo/toc`) and keeps its engagement with the training ships. The tactical scanner fills the window and the controls float over it. The page scales as one piece from an iPad in landscape (the smallest size) to 4K; it is not laid out for portrait. `tests/tactical-officer-layout.cjs` checks those sizes, `tests/tactical-officer.cjs` the behaviour and `tests/tactical-weapons.cjs` the weapon set-up overlays.

Files: `index.html`, `styles.css`, `tactical.js` and `camera.js`, minified from `demo-app/static-src/` by `node scripts/build-static.cjs`. It also uses `../Shared/console.css` and `../Shared/console-ui.js` (the console look and controls), `../Shared/nav-world.js` (the chart) and `../Shared/view-screen-settings.js` (the bridge view screen's settings).

- **Tactical scanner (the whole window):** heading up, as on the Helm's SRS view: our ship at the centre of a compass rose in relative bearings, spokes every 30°, range rings with their distances, and the chart's bodies and stations. Our four shield quadrants show as arcs on the rose and the view screen camera's field as an amber wedge. Training ships are chevrons: green while unknown, red once identified as enemies (a dashed ring marks the 100 km identification distance), and enemies beyond the window are marked at its edge. The selected target pulses and carries hull and shield bars. Shots, beams, shield flashes, explosions and destroyed ships are drawn where they happen. **SRS / LRS** in the top bar; the **range** slider on the right (1 km to 1 BKM on SRS, 1 BKM to 30 LY on LRS) or the mouse wheel. Tap a contact to select it.
- **Weapons (left):** the fire control: six buttons, red when ready to fire: phaser cannons 1 and 2 (forward ±45°), torpedo 1 (forward ±30°), torpedo 2 (aft ±30°), phaser array 1 (all round, 100 km) and phaser cannon 3 (aft ±45°). Only an armed weapon fires: each shows SAFE (not armed), TUBE EMPTY, LOADING, CHARGING, NO TARGET, OUT OF RANGE, OUT OF ARC or READY, with its charge or load bar. Weapons start safe and the tubes empty; arm them from **Torpedoes**, **EPW** and **EBW** in the bottom bar.
- **Shields (left):** our four quadrants as a ring around the hull with their SEO, whether the shields are on, the total, the generator, and the hull.
- **Target (right):** enemy or unknown, distance, speed, bearing and turn, and after a detailed scan the hull, weapons (armed or safe), people aboard and warp coils; its four shield quadrants always; the scan's progress; and the targeted subsystem, LOCKED (gold) or NOT LOCKED (amber).
- **Advisory line:** ship destroyed, hull critical, shields off, incoming fire, the target and how many weapons are ready, scanning.
- **Bottom bar:** **Scan Target**, **Subsystems** (the target's nine subsystems once scanned; tap one to target it at any range, tap again to clear), **Clear Target**, **Torpedoes**, **EPW** and **EBW** (the weapon set-up overlays below; red while any of their weapons is armed), **Shields** (on and off: green when on, red when off), **View Screen** (the camera) and **Condition** (readiness I to V, also shown in the top bar). With nine commands the side columns end above the bar.
- **Weapon set-up (Torpedoes, EPW, EBW):** for a ship short of weapons officers, every weapon can be run from here. Each button opens an overlay with a card for each of the ship's weapons of that kind (state at a glance); tap one for its set-up, **‹** goes back to the list, **✕**, Escape or a tap outside closes it. A message for the overlay shows inside it as well as in the toast.
  - **Torpedoes** (Torpedo 1 forward, Torpedo 2 aft): the cards show the tube (empty, loading, loaded or armed), the ready rack (n / 6), a round assembling, the warheads left and the three auto switches. The set-up is **warheads only**: **Yield** (0–100%, the Torpedo Assembly slider; WED 30 and WKD 150 at 100%), **Trigger** (**Impact**, or **Proximity and impact**) and, with proximity, **Distance from target** on the keypad (metres; Escape steps back from the keypad). Under it: **Assemble** (one round at a time, 10 s, from the launcher's own stock of 12 hulls and 8 warheads, into the ready rack of six), and the switches **Auto assemble** (keeps six rounds assembled to the current set-up; when one is loaded into the tube it builds another), **Auto load** (an empty tube takes the next round from the rack, 2.5 s) and **Auto arm** (a loaded round is armed). The launcher side shows the assembly's progress, the six rack slots with each round's yield and trigger, the tube with **Load** and **Arm / Safe**, the stock, and the weapon's fire-control state. Changing the set-up affects only rounds assembled after it. With all three switches on the launcher feeds itself: after each shot the next round comes through and the weapons panel shows it ready again. A blocked command (rack full, nothing to load, out of stock) says why.
  - **EPW** (phase cannons 1, 2 and 3) and **EBW** (phaser array 1): the cards show armed or safe, the shot power and damage, and the charge. The set-up has **Shot power** (1–100%, as on EPW and EBW Control; damage and recharge scale with it), the chamber or capacitor charge, firing arc, range and recharge time, the fire-control state, and **Arm / Safe**.
- **View Screen camera:** a joystick like the Helm's, with two axes: left and right swing the camera around the ship (a full 360°, up to 90° a second), up and down zoom in and out (1× to 20×, up to 2^1.2 a second). The further it is pushed the faster it goes; let go and it springs back and holds; the arrow keys push it halfway. **Forward**, **Starboard**, **Aft** and **Port** jump straight to those views. A dial shows the ship from above with the camera's field, and the bearing and zoom are read out. The bridge View Screen follows at once.
- **Menu → Demo controls:** stand-ins kept off the console: enemy impulse and behaviour (attack or run), add a random training ship (up to eight), charge the target's warp coils, enemy contacts and enemies known on entry; our impulse and navigation (follow the enemy or move away); the detailed scan's distance and time, people aboard and whether the target is armed; and every weapon setting (power or yield, speed, targeting range, the cannon's full-power and dissipation distances, the torpedo's lifetime; cannon and array power set every cannon's or array's shot power, and torpedo yield is the training ships'); **Weapons ready on entry** (off at first) arms every energy weapon and gives each tube an armed round, three in its rack and auto load and auto arm on, now and at each reset. **Reset demo** starts again.

## Weapons fire (2026-11-02)

The page already draws our own shots and the training ships' fire at us on the SRS; the scanner (SRS and LRS) adds the stand-in exchange between two other ships (amber), so no shot is drawn twice. The demo has no simulator, so `../Shared/weapons-fire.js` fires stand-in volleys: two seconds after the page opens, every 15 seconds after that, and on demand from Demo controls → **Fire a test volley**. In each, a hostile drill ship fires a torpedo and pulses at us (red), we fire a pulse and a torpedo back (cyan) and two other drill ships exchange a beam and a pulse (amber); about eight seconds. A torpedo is a diamond with a short trail and a label, a pulse a bolt fading over 0.6 s, a beam a line held for its length then fading, a detonation an expanding ring — the look of the app's `ship-web/src/app/shared/weapons-fire.ts`. The drill ships are placed at fractions of each view's range ahead of our ship (turned to our heading when the volley starts, and moving with us), so a volley shows at any range. `?fire=0` turns the automatic volleys off; `DemoFire.drawn[view]` holds what each view drew in its latest frame (`tests/weapons-fire.cjs`).

## Rules (from the Angular TOC)

- The simulation steps ten times a second. Impulse is 20,000 m/s at 100%; turn rate is 3°/s up to 56% impulse, falling to none at 75%.
- **Our flight:** we steer at the selected ship, or the first surviving one (or directly away from it), at our impulse.
- **Training ships** attack (head for us, break off at 200 m, come back from 5 km) or run. Any downed shield quadrant sends them away, turning off a downed aft quadrant. With the impulse engine out a ship coasts to a stop in 10 seconds. They fire their torpedo (50 km, ±30°), cannon (25 km, ±45°) and array (100 km) at us at half our power settings, unless their sensors are out; running ships fire only once their impulse engine is out.
- **Our weapons:** cannons 120 WED / 40 WKD a pulse at 15,000 m/s, recharging 1.5 s (both scaled by shot power), full damage to 50,000 km and none at 90,000 km; torpedoes 30 / 150 at 100% yield at 10,000 m/s for 60 s, loading 2.5 s, one round in the tube at a time; the array 60 / 60 a second for one second, recharging 2 s, falling from full at 50 km to a tenth at 100 km. Every shot is led to where the target will be, using its velocity relative to ours. A proximity warhead goes off where its course first passes within its distance of a ship's hull (or on impact, if that comes first), and hits the quadrant facing it.
- **Hits:** a shot meeting a shield quadrant that is up drains it; otherwise it damages the hull and a subsystem on that side (the targeted one if there). Within 5 km a shot at a targeted subsystem strikes it alone. Losing the reactor may destroy the ship. Training ships have 50 SEO a quadrant (recovering 10 every 15 s) and a 500 hull.
- **Scans** reach 30,000 km and take 3 s, three times as long while any shield quadrant is up.
- **Subsystem targeting:** once a ship is scanned, a subsystem can be picked at any range. The pick locks only while the ship is within 25 km (announced as the lock is gained or lost), and shots aim at it only while it is locked; within 5 km a locked shot strikes it alone.
- **Our shields:** 100 SEO a quadrant, recovering 10 a second while on; the quadrant facing the shooter (measured from our bow) takes the hit, and with it down or the shields off the hull does. At zero hull the simulation ends.

## Changes from the Angular page

- The enemy's hits land on the quadrant facing the shooter from our bow (the Angular page ignored our heading).
- The lead allows for our own motion, the array's beam stays on its target, and a miss-tap on LRS no longer re-selects an older pick.
- A body's distance and bearing are measured from where we are now.
- The scanner opens at 200 km so the training ships 110 km out are in view.
- Shields on and off moved from the shields panel to the bottom bar.
- A subsystem can be picked as soon as the scan completes; it locks within 25 km (the Angular page refused the pick until then).
- Weapons are armed from Tactical's own overlays (Torpedoes, EPW, EBW) and fire only when armed; torpedoes come one round at a time from each launcher's rack and tube instead of reloading without end. The Angular page had no arming or launcher set-up.
