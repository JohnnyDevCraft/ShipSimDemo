# View Screen

The bridge's main screen (2026-10-29), on the Helm design and built around the weapon consoles' forward targeting scope. It is a display: nothing on it takes input. Communications Control sets it up, Tactical Officer Control points its camera and zooms, and the Helm flies the ship it follows. The page scales as one piece from an iPad in landscape (the smallest size) to 4K; it is not laid out for portrait. `tests/view-screen-layout.cjs` checks those sizes and `tests/view-screen.cjs` the behaviour.

Files: `index.html`, `styles.css` and `view.js`, minified with `../Shared/view-screen-settings.js` from `demo-app/static-src/` by `node scripts/build-static.cjs`. It also uses `../Shared/console.css` (the console look), `../Shared/nav-world.js` (the chart), `../Shared/nav-computer.js` (our ship's position from the Helm, and the destination) and `../Shared/scene.js` (the 3D Earth, Moon and Sun).

- **Scope (the whole window):** the view along the camera: dead ahead unless the tactical officer turns it (FORWARD, STARBOARD, AFT, PORT or any bearing from the bow, shown in the top bar and under the heading scale), drawn like the weapon consoles' scope: stars, the 3D models, the horizon, the reticle, and a heading scale across the top. Bodies, stations and nearby ships are drawn where they are, at their true size for the zoom, with their names and distances; ships closer than 500 km show as hulls. The zoom (1× to 20×) comes from Communications' presets or the tactical officer's joystick and glides to a new setting; small camera turns glide, big ones swing straight there. Looking aft at warp, the stars stream away.
- **At warp:** stars stream past from dead ahead, the edges glow blue and WARP and the factor show over the reticle. The objects ahead stay on the scope and grow as the ship closes on them: warping toward Earth, Earth grows until the ship drops out at its safe distance.
- **Scanners:** short-range bottom left, long-range bottom right, each centred on our ship, north up, with range rings, the bearing scale, a wedge for what the forward scope can see, the destination (amber diamond) and the tactical target (cyan box). Communications chooses SRS, LRS, both (DUAL) or NONE (the forward scope alone), the two ranges, and which objects and labels show.
- **Left column:** the tactical target (kind, name, range, bearing, and whether it is on the screen; on the scope it is bracketed, or marked at the edge with the turn to it) and docking (docked, moorings, clamps, umbilical, power sync).
- **Right column:** navigation: the destination held by the navigation computer, its distance, the course and turn to it, the time to arrive at the current speed, our heading, and the warp drive and impulse lights. The destination is marked on the scope too.
- **Advisory line:** reactor temperature critical, at warp and the time to go, the tactical target, docked, impulse or holding.
- **Gauges** (where the other consoles keep their commands): bus load, reactor output (with the requested load and efficiency), reactor temperature, and speed (km/s at impulse, the warp factor at warp).
- **Menu → Demo controls:** stand-ins kept off the screen itself. With the Helm closed, a stand-in ship flies here: fly toward an object under impulse, or warp in from a set distance; all stop; reset. Also the tactical target, bus load, reactor output and temperature, docking and power sync.

## Rules

- **Settings** live in storage that both pages read (`xmps-demo-view-screen`): view type, SRS and LRS ranges, label and object filters, scope zoom, and the camera bearing from the bow. A change on Communications reaches an open View Screen at once. The default is DUAL at 1×.
- **Following the Helm:** the Helm reports our position, heading, course, speed, turn rate, warp factor, where a warp jump ends, and its docking phase, once a second. A report older than 90 seconds is ignored and the stand-in takes over.
- **Twenty times a second** the screen refreshes its world: our position, the other objects, the navigation computer, the panels and the scanners. Between the Helm's reports and between refreshes, every frame **plays ahead** from the last report: along its course at its speed, turning at its turn rate for up to two seconds, and never past the end of a warp jump. A new report that disagrees a little with the play-ahead is eased in over a fraction of a second instead of jumping.
- The scope's field is 600 units across at a focal length of 520 × zoom, as on the weapon consoles.
- Reactor, bus and power sync values are demo stand-ins; tactical selection will come from the tactical station.
