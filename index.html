STARSHIP BRIDGE SIMULATION

Explore ShipsMS

ShipsMS is a starship bridge simulator where a crew works together to navigate space, manage engineering systems, communicate with other ships and stations, and carry out missions. Each station has its own controls, while the full simulator keeps everyone connected to the same ship.

Behind the bridge are power and propulsion systems, shields, weapons, transporters, and a living mission setting. AI-driven contacts provide dialogue and mission details, while the simulation software applies the rules for movement, equipment, and combat.

These interactive demos run locally in your browser with sample data. They do not connect to a running simulation or affect a player’s ship. They are previews under active development, not a complete multiplayer game.

Bridge & engineering

Helm Control ↗Fly the ship from a full-screen scanner: impulse and warp, joystick heading, docking, and course and galactic plots, aligned to the destination navigation sets. Navigation Control ↗Set destinations from a full-screen system map, drop and edit a free point, and plot courses for other ships or from any known body. Open it beside Helm Control to see them share one navigation computer. Reactor Control ↗Run the ship’s fusion reactor from a full-screen schematic: fuel, confinement, collectors and coolant, with damage, faults and repairs. Battery Control ↗Watch the backup battery charge and supply the ship bus, switch recharge and bus power, and monitor power sync with a docked station. Power Distribution ↗Switch and regulate every powered subsystem, watch the hull bus temperature, and check the bus and battery gauges. Communications ↗Explore transmissions, frequencies, and contact controls. Transporter Control ↗Beam personnel and cargo from a full-screen transfer schematic: scan, lock, dematerialize into the pattern buffer and materialize at the destination, with range, shield and power interlocks. View Screen ↗The forward scope with both scanners, set up from Communications.

Weapons & defense

Tactical Officer Control ↗Track contacts, scan subsystems, and coordinate weapons and shields. Torpedo Assembly ↗Prepare warheads, probes, and beacon payloads. Torpedo Launch Control ↗Load, arm, aim, and launch prepared torpedoes. Phase Cannon ↗Aim and fire traveling energy pulses. Phaser Array ↗Experiment with beam targeting, charge, and power. Shield System Control ↗Defend the ship from a full-screen shield envelope: four facings, extension and allocation, enemy bearings on a compass rose, hits, recovery and cover for nearby ships.

No account or simulation server required. Demo state is local to the page; refreshing may reset it.
