STARSHIP BRIDGE SIMULATION

Explore ShipsMS

ShipsMS is a starship bridge simulator where a crew works together to navigate space, manage engineering systems, communicate with other ships and stations, and carry out missions. Each station has its own controls, while the full simulator keeps everyone connected to the same ship.

Behind the bridge are power and propulsion systems, shields, weapons, transporters, and a living mission setting. AI-driven contacts provide dialogue and mission details, while the simulation software applies the rules for movement, equipment, and combat.

These interactive demos run locally in your browser with sample data. They do not connect to a running simulation or affect a player’s ship. They are previews under active development, not a complete multiplayer game.

Bridge & engineering

Weapons & defense

Tactical Officer Control ↗Track contacts, scan subsystems, and coordinate weapons and shields. Torpedo Assembly ↗Prepare warheads, probes, and beacon payloads. Torpedo Launch Control ↗Load, arm, aim, and launch prepared torpedoes. Phase Cannon ↗Aim and fire traveling energy pulses. Phaser Array ↗Experiment with beam targeting, charge, and power. Shield System Control ↗Manage shield quadrants, output, and recovery.

No account or simulation server required. Demo state is local to the page; refreshing may reset it.
